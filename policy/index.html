Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how USRATI handles information when you use the USRATI mobile application. USRATI is designed as a family-focused application for organising daily journeys, routines, family tasks, prayer-aware activities, guidance resources and personal growth.

USRATI is designed to keep core family and journey information on the user's device. Some features may require optional device permissions, such as location or access to media selected by the user.

1. Information USRATI May Process

Depending on the features you choose to use, USRATI may process information such as:

  • Family member names, roles, avatars and profile settings entered by you.
  • Daily journey tasks, routines, family tasks, milestones, completion records and related settings.
  • Prayer settings and a configured location used to calculate prayer times.
  • Location information when you explicitly enable a location-based feature such as Follow My Location.
  • Images, videos, written guides or other resources that you choose to attach inside the application.
  • App preferences and locally stored configuration required for USRATI to function.

2. Location Information

USRATI may use location information to support prayer-time calculations when a location-based mode is enabled. Location access is not intended for advertising, profiling or continuous tracking.

If Follow My Location is enabled, the application may request the device's current location when the app resumes so that prayer settings can be updated after meaningful travel. USRATI does not require continuous background GPS tracking for this feature.

You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. When location access is unavailable, USRATI may continue using a previously configured or manually selected location where supported.

3. Local Storage

USRATI stores application data on the user's device to provide its features. This may include family profiles, tasks, routines, journey completions, settings, milestones and media that the user intentionally adds to the app.

Locally stored data remains on the device unless removed through an available delete/reset feature, cleared by the operating system, or removed when the application is uninstalled.

4. Media and Guidance Resources

USRATI may allow users to add images, videos, written guidance or links as resources for daily tasks. Media selected from the device may be copied into application-controlled storage so that the feature continues to work reliably.

USRATI does not use user-selected media for advertising. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to use or share any content they add to the application.

5. Children's and Family Information

USRATI is designed for family use and may include profiles for children. A parent or guardian should supervise the creation and management of a child's profile and the information entered into the application.

USRATI does not knowingly sell children's personal information or use children's information for personalised advertising.

6. Period Mode and Sensitive Settings

USRATI may provide optional private settings such as Period Mode for relevant family profiles. This feature is manually controlled by the user and is not intended to perform medical tracking, cycle prediction, health diagnosis or automated inference.

7. Device Permissions

USRATI may request permissions only when required by an enabled feature. These may include:

  • Location permission for location-aware prayer settings.
  • Photo or video access when you choose media from your device.
  • Notification permission where reminders or prayer notifications are enabled.

You may manage or revoke these permissions through your device settings.

8. Third-Party Services and External Links

USRATI may rely on operating-system services, software libraries or external links selected by users. When you leave USRATI or interact with a third-party service, that provider's own privacy practices may apply.

USRATI is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or availability of independent third-party services.

9. Data Sharing and Sale

USRATI does not sell personal information. USRATI does not share personal information with advertisers for behavioural or personalised advertising.

Information may be disclosed if required by applicable law, legal process or a valid governmental request, or where reasonably necessary to protect users, the developer or the security of the application.

10. Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect information handled by USRATI. However, no application, device, electronic storage system or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

Users should protect access to their device and use the device security features provided by their operating system.

11. Data Deletion

Where the application provides a Delete All Local Data or similar function, users may use it to remove locally stored USRATI data. Uninstalling the application may also remove app-controlled local data, subject to the behaviour of the device operating system and any backups controlled by the user.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to USRATI, applicable requirements or privacy practices. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date.
